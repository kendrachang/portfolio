Projects

Scotch-Yoke Weight Lifter

This device uses a scotch-yoke linkage to convert the rotary motion of a hand crank into the linear motion needed to lift a weight. The frame combines laser-cut acrylic panels with 3D-printed fittings, designed and modeled in Fusion 360.

Fusion 360 3D printing Laser cutting Mechanism design
02

Ping-Pong Ball Launcher

A ball-launching mechanism designed to maximize projectile distance while staying in place against only a minimal counterweight. It holds five to ten balls at a time, feeding them through a funnel and rotating disk for quick reloading between launches, and fires each one with a spring-loaded trigger mechanism.

CAD 3D printing Assembly Mechanism design
03

Laser-Cut Takeout Box

A plywood takeout box modeled in Fusion 360 and cut on a laser cutter, designed entirely from interlocking tab-and-slot joints rather than glue or fasteners. The project was a hands-on exercise in kerf compensation — tuning tolerances so the laser's cut width didn't leave joints too loose or too tight to assemble.

Fusion 360 Laser cutting Tolerancing
04

Mass-Optimized Headphone Holder

A headphone holder refined through hand-calculated stress analysis and iterative FEA, cutting material mass by 96.2%, cost by 83%, and print time by 73% compared to the initial design while still holding the load. The final bracket was fabricated and load-tested using FDM 3D printing.

FEA Hand calculations FDM 3D printing Design optimization
